Privacy policy

Last updated 5 September 2026

Slopstop collects no personal data, transmits none, and stores nothing about you anywhere but your own device.

What is collected

Nothing. Slopstop has no analytics, no accounts, no crash reporting, no telemetry and no servers operated by us. No information about you or your listening is gathered, sold, shared or transmitted.

What is stored, and where

Everything Slopstop saves lives in your browser's local extension storage on your own device:

  • The artists, songs and keywords you have chosen to block.
  • A cached copy of the public AI-artist lists, so they are not re-downloaded on every page load.

None of this is synced to us or to anyone else. Removing the extension removes it. You can export it to a file at any time from the extension popup, and that file goes only where you put it.

Network requests

Slopstop makes requests to exactly two addresses, both to download public lists of AI-generated artists. Both are plain downloads: they carry no identifier, no listening history, and nothing about you.

AddressPurpose
raw.githubusercontent.com Fetches the community AI-artist list maintained by the spotify-ai-blocker project.
zoundhub.com Fetches a supplementary AI-artist list.

These are ordinary downloads, so the hosts see your IP address as they would for any web page you open. That is the extent of it. Requests happen at most once every six hours, or when you press Sync.

What Slopstop does to your YouTube Music account

When a blocked track is detected, Slopstop presses thumbs down on it and then skips to the next track. This is a real change to your YouTube Music account, made deliberately so the recommendation algorithm learns from it. It happens only for tracks matching your blocklists, and every instance is written to the browser console.

Slopstop does not read, modify or transmit anything else in your account.

Children

Slopstop is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date at the top will change with it. Since no data is collected, any change would be a clarification rather than a new use of your information.
