Privacy policy

Last updated 5 September 2026

Slopstop collects no personal data, transmits none, and stores nothing about you anywhere except your own device.

What is collected

Nothing. There is no analytics, no account system, no crash reporting and no telemetry, and there are no servers operated by me. No information about you or your listening is gathered, sold, shared or transmitted.

What is stored, and where

Everything Slopstop saves lives in your browser's local extension storage:

  • The artists, songs and keywords you have chosen to block.
  • A cached copy of the public AI artist lists, so they are not downloaded again on every page load.

None of it is synced anywhere. Uninstalling the extension removes it. You can export your lists to a file from the extension popup, and that file goes only where you put it.

Network requests

Slopstop contacts exactly two addresses, both to download public lists of AI-generated artists. Both are plain file downloads carrying no identifier and no listening history.

AddressPurpose
raw.githubusercontent.com The community AI artist list maintained by the spotify-ai-blocker project.
zoundhub.com A supplementary AI artist list.

As with opening any web page, those hosts can see your IP address. That is the extent of what they receive. Requests happen at most once every six hours, or when you press Sync in the popup.

Changes made to your YouTube Music account

When a blocked track starts, Slopstop presses thumbs down on it and then skips to the next track. That is a real change to your account, made on purpose so the recommendation algorithm stops offering similar material. It happens only for tracks matching your blocklists, and every instance is written to the browser console.

Slopstop reads, changes and transmits nothing else in your account.

Children

Slopstop is not directed at children, and collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Since no data is collected, any change would be a clarification rather than a new use of your information.
