Slopstop

Stop AI slop from hijacking your listening.

What it does

Streaming catalogues are filling up with mass-produced AI tracks. Slopstop keeps them out of your listening automatically.

  • Blocks known AI artists from community-maintained databases, refreshed in the background.
  • Downvotes and skips the moment a blocked track starts, so the algorithm learns too.
  • Block your own artists, songs, or title keywords — "Sped Up", "Nightcore", whatever grates.
  • Tells you why. Every block is logged with the term it matched and which list it came from.

Careful about false positives

An artist name is only ever matched against the artist, never the album or track title. Single-word names must match exactly, so an AI act called "Angel" will not take out Angel Olsen, and "Nova" will not touch Nova Twins.

Install

Not in the stores yet. Slopstop is awaiting review on Firefox Add-ons and the Chrome Web Store. Until then you can load it manually — it takes about a minute.

Firefox

  • Download the extension folder.
  • Open about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox
  • Click Load Temporary Add-on… and pick manifest.json

Requires Firefox 142 or newer. Temporary add-ons are removed when Firefox closes.

Chrome

  • Download the extension folder.
  • Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode
  • Click Load unpacked and pick the folder

Privacy

Slopstop collects nothing. There are no analytics, no accounts, no servers of ours, and no telemetry of any kind.

  • Your blocklists are stored on your own device and never leave it.
  • The only network requests fetch two public AI-artist lists. They send no information about you — not what you listen to, not who you are.
  • Nothing you do in the extension is reported anywhere.

The full policy is on the privacy policy page.
